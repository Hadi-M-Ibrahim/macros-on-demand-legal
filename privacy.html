Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Macros On Demand handles information when you use the iOS app. The app helps you generate restaurant meal ideas around macro targets and optional restaurant/location preferences.

Information The App May Process

  • Nutrition preferences and app settings. Macro targets, calorie targets, restaurant preferences, hidden restaurants, maximum distance, generated recommendations, and saved meal identifiers may be stored locally on your device. If a submitted build enables optional account sync and you choose to use it, selected goals, preferences, and saved meals may also be synced through Supabase.
  • Account information. The default build does not require an account. If a submitted build enables optional account sync and you choose to use Sign in with Apple, Apple may provide a user identifier and, depending on your Apple choice, your name and email address. Supabase may be used to authenticate and sync account data for configured sync builds.
  • Purchase information. Premium purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store and by RevenueCat for entitlement management. The app may receive product identifiers, subscription status, entitlement status, transaction metadata, and RevenueCat customer identifiers. The app does not receive your full payment card number.
  • Location search information. If you grant location permission, the app may use your approximate current location to rank nearby restaurants. You can also search by city or ZIP code, or use national results without device location. Location lookup may use Apple location and maps services. Recent location search context may be stored locally to preserve app state.
  • Analytics events. If cloud services are configured, the app may record product events such as app open, onboarding completion, order generation, saved or unsaved meals, restaurant preference changes, account sync, catalog review, purchase completion, restore, and RevenueCat account-link status. These events may include event properties and, when signed in, a user identifier.
  • Support information. If you contact support by email, the contents of your message and reply address are used to respond. If you contact support through GitHub Issues, the information you choose to post may be publicly visible on GitHub.

How Information Is Used

  • Provide meal recommendations, saved meals, restaurant preferences, Premium access, and optional account sync when enabled and chosen.
  • Operate RevenueCat entitlement checks, App Store purchase restore flows, and, for configured sync builds, Sign in with Apple and Supabase sync.
  • Rank nearby restaurants when you enable location-based search.
  • Understand app behavior, troubleshoot issues, improve catalog quality, and verify that purchase and sync integrations are working.
  • Respond to support requests and comply with legal, security, or platform obligations.

Third-Party Services

The app may rely on third-party services that process information under their own terms and policies:

  • Apple. App Store purchases, device location permissions, geocoding, maps, subscription management, refunds, Apple account services, and Sign in with Apple if account sync is enabled for the submitted build.
  • Supabase. Optional authentication, account profile data, preference sync, saved meal sync, catalog review records, and analytics event storage when configured for the submitted build.
  • RevenueCat. Premium entitlement status, offering/product metadata, subscription status, purchase restore state, and customer identifiers.
  • GitHub. Public documentation hosting through GitHub Pages and support requests submitted through GitHub Issues.

The app does not use personal information for third-party behavioral advertising based on the current implementation described above.

Your Choices

  • You can use core app features without signing in. Account sync is not required for the default build. For builds that enable sync, account sync requires Sign in with Apple and production Supabase configuration.
  • You can deny location permission and use city/ZIP search or national results instead.
  • You can opt out of optional app analytics from the Account privacy controls.
  • You can manage or cancel App Store subscriptions through your Apple account. Refund eligibility is handled by Apple.
  • You can delete local app data by deleting the app from your device. In-app cloud account deletion is available only for builds where production Supabase configuration and the delete-account function are deployed and verified. Cloud account deletion or correction follow-up can also be requested through the support page.

Data Retention

Local data remains on your device until you change app settings, clear data through app controls where available, or delete the app. If optional account sync is enabled and used, synced account data may remain in Supabase until it is deleted through deployed app functionality, support action, or backend retention processes. Purchase records are retained by Apple and RevenueCat according to their own requirements and operational needs.

Children

Macros On Demand is not intended for children under 13, and the app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Nutrition And Health Context

The app provides general nutrition information and restaurant meal recommendations. It is not medical advice, dietetic advice, allergy advice, or a substitute for a qualified professional. Restaurant ingredients, preparation, availability, and nutrition values can vary.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, use the support page or email macrosondemand.mod@gmail.com. Avoid posting sensitive personal information in a public GitHub issue.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the app, backend services, or App Store configuration changes. Material updates should be reviewed against the shipped build and provider dashboards.
