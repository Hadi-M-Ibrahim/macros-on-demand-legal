Support

Use this page for Macros On Demand support, privacy questions, optional account sync questions if enabled, and Premium purchase troubleshooting.

Open a support request Email support Apple billing support

GitHub Issues may be public. Use email for account-specific or privacy-sensitive requests. Do not include payment details, access tokens, private health details, or precise location data in a public support request.

Contact

For private support, privacy requests, cloud data deletion follow-up, or App Store review questions, email macrosondemand.mod@gmail.com.

Public product issues and catalog corrections can also be filed through GitHub Issues.

What To Include

  • The app version and iOS version, if available.
  • Whether account sync was enabled for your build and, if so, whether you were signed in with Apple or using the app as a guest.
  • For Premium issues, the plan you attempted to buy and whether Restore Purchases was tried.
  • For nutrition/catalog issues, the restaurant, item name, and what looked incorrect.
  • For location search issues, whether you used current location, city, or ZIP search. Do not post precise home addresses.

Premium Purchase Help

Premium access is purchased through the App Store and checked in the app through RevenueCat. If Premium does not unlock after purchase, open the app's Premium screen and use Restore Purchases. Make sure you are using the same Apple account that made the purchase.

Subscription cancellation, refund requests, unfamiliar charges, and payment-method issues are handled by Apple through Apple billing support.

Privacy Or Data Requests

For privacy questions, cloud sync deletion requests if you used sync, or correction requests, email support. If you use GitHub Issues, avoid posting sensitive information publicly and ask for a private follow-up path instead.
